<div class="article-section mb-5"><h2>How Do Latitude and Longitude Work?</h2><p>Latitude tells you how far north or south a location is from the Equator. </p><p>Longitude tells you how far east or west a location is from the Prime Meridian. </p><p>Together, latitude and longitude identify a position on Earth. </p><div class="alert alert-primary"><strong>Example:</strong>A GPS device can provide a latitude and longitude pair that represents a specific location. You can then use those coordinates with a map, distance calculator, address lookup, or another geolocation tool. </div></div><div class="article-section mb-5"><h2>What Is Geocoding?</h2><p>Geocoding is the process of converting an address or place name into geographic coordinates such as latitude and longitude. </p>
<div class="row g-3 my-3"><div class="col-md-6"><div class="card border-0 bg-light text-center"><div class="card-body p-4"><h3 class="h5">Geocoding</h3><p class="mb-0 fw-semibold">Address → Latitude + Longitude </p></div></div></div>
<div class="col-md-6"><div class="card border-0 bg-light text-center"><div class="card-body p-4"><h3 class="h5">Reverse Geocoding</h3><p class="mb-0 fw-semibold">Latitude + Longitude → Address </p></div></div></div></div><p class="mb-0">Both processes are widely used in maps, navigation, delivery applications, location services, and geographic information systems. </p></div><div class="article-section mb-5"><h2>What Is the Difference Between GPS and Geolocation?</h2><p>GPS is a satellite-based positioning system that can determine the location of a device. </p><p>Geolocation is a broader term for identifying or estimating a geographic location. It can use GPS, IP addresses, Wi-Fi, mobile networks, browser location services, or other sources. </p><div class="alert alert-info"><strong>In simple words:</strong>GPS is one way to obtain location information, while geolocation can involve several different technologies. </div></div>
<div class="article-section mb-5"><h2>Are These Geolocation Tools Accurate?</h2><p>Accuracy depends on the type of information being used. </p><p>GPS-based coordinates can be quite precise when a device has a good satellite signal. IP-based location is generally less precise and may identify a broader area rather than an exact address. </p><p class="mb-0">Map data, coordinate formats, and the underlying location service can also affect results. For important surveying, engineering, legal, or professional work, always verify results with an appropriate authoritative source. </p></div>
