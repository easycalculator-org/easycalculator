How Do Latitude and Longitude Work?

Latitude tells you how far north or south a location is from the Equator.

Longitude tells you how far east or west a location is from the Prime Meridian.

Together, latitude and longitude identify a position on Earth.

Example:A GPS device can provide a latitude and longitude pair that represents a specific location. You can then use those coordinates with a map, distance calculator, address lookup, or another geolocation tool.

What Is Geocoding?

Geocoding is the process of converting an address or place name into geographic coordinates such as latitude and longitude.

Geocoding

Address → Latitude + Longitude

Reverse Geocoding

Latitude + Longitude → Address

Both processes are widely used in maps, navigation, delivery applications, location services, and geographic information systems.

What Is the Difference Between GPS and Geolocation?

GPS is a satellite-based positioning system that can determine the location of a device.

Geolocation is a broader term for identifying or estimating a geographic location. It can use GPS, IP addresses, Wi-Fi, mobile networks, browser location services, or other sources.

In simple words:GPS is one way to obtain location information, while geolocation can involve several different technologies.

Are These Geolocation Tools Accurate?

Accuracy depends on the type of information being used.

GPS-based coordinates can be quite precise when a device has a good satellite signal. IP-based location is generally less precise and may identify a broader area rather than an exact address.

Map data, coordinate formats, and the underlying location service can also affect results. For important surveying, engineering, legal, or professional work, always verify results with an appropriate authoritative source.

A ZIP Code(Zone Improvement Plan code) is a 5- or 9-digit number used by postal services to identify a geographic delivery area. Introduced by the U.S. Postal Service (USPS)in 1963, ZIP codes improve mail sorting and delivery.
