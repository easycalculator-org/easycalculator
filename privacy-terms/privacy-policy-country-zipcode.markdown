Location and address lookup</h2><p>With your permission, the app accesses your approximate or precise location while you use it to display your coordinates, postal code, address and position on a map. It may refresh your location when you open the app if permission has already been granted.</p><p>Android location and geocoding services may process your coordinates to determine your position and convert it into an address. Offline searches</h2><p class="mb-0">ZIP code, city, state and USA telephone area-code searches use data bundled with the app. These search queries are processed on your phone and are not sent to our server. Opening an online map or boundary makes the separate requests described below.</p></div></section><section class="card shadow-sm mb-3"><div class="card-body p-4"><h2 class="h5 fw-bold mb-3">3. Saved locations</h2><p>When you choose to save a location, the app stores its name, latitude, longitude, location accuracy, available address and saved time in private app storage on your phone. You can save up to 50 locations, rename them or delete them.</p><p>The app does not upload these saved records to our servers or Firebase. Opening a saved place on a map requests map tiles for that area. A saved place represents the recorded position, not continuous tracking.</p><div class="alert alert-secondary mb-0">Saved locations remain until you delete them, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. The supplied upgrade disables Android app backup and app-data transfer. </div></div></section><section class="card shadow-sm mb-3"><div class="card-body p-4"><h2 class="h5 fw-bold mb-3">4. Maps and ZIP boundaries</h2><p>The app uses OpenStreetMap tiles and U.S. Census Bureau TIGERweb ZIP Code Tabulation Area boundaries. Network requests disclose your IP address and technical request details to the respective providers. Map tile requests identify the displayed geographic area; boundary requests identify the selected ZIP code.</p><p>These providers may process request logs under their own policies:</p><ul><li><a href="https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy" target="_blank" rel="noopener">OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy</a></li><li><a href="https://www.census.gov/about/policies/privacy.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">U.S. Census Bureau Privacy Policy</a></li></ul><div class="alert alert-warning mb-0">Census boundaries are approximate statistical ZIP areas, not official USPS delivery boundaries. Location and address results may be inaccurate and should <strong>not</strong>be relied on for emergency services. </div></div></section><section class="card shadow-sm mb-3"><div class="card-body p-4"><h2 class="h5 fw-bold mb-3">5. Firebase processes app installation identifiers, messaging registration tokens and technical connection or delivery information needed to provide these services. Network connections also expose an IP address to the service provider.</p><p>The app subscribes to its announcement topic. The Notifications page displays the five most recent available messages and caches message information on the phone. We do not send your saved locations, location names, GPS coordinates or offline search queries to Firebase through this app.</p><div class="alert alert-info">You can disable system notifications in Android settings. Disabling alerts does not disable Firebase registration or prevent the Notifications page from fetching messages when opened. </div><p class="mb-0">For Google's handling and retention of service data, see <a href="https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Privacy and Security in Firebase</a>and the <a href="https://policies.google.com/privacy" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Google Privacy Policy</a>.</p></div></section><section class="card shadow-sm mb-3"><div class="card-body p-4"><h2 class="h5 fw-bold mb-3">6.
