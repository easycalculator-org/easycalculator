<section><h2>How to convert CST to EST</h2><p>To convert <strong>CST to EST, add 1 hour</strong>. For example, 9:00 AM CST becomes 10:00 AM EST. The minutes and seconds do not change. This simple rule helps you read event schedules, plan calls and compare times across the Central and Eastern standard time zones.</p><p class="ce-formula">EST = CST + 1 hour</p><p>Think of two clocks. The Eastern standard-time clock is one hour ahead of the Central standard-time clock. When the Central clock shows 4:15:30 PM, the Eastern clock shows 5:15:30 PM.</p>

<section><h2>CST to EST examples</h2><h3>Example 1: an online lesson</h3><p>A lesson begins at <strong>9:00 AM CST</strong>. Add one hour: 9:00 AM + 1 hour = <strong>10:00 AM EST</strong>. The date stays the same.</p><h3>Example 2: an afternoon call</h3><p>A call is scheduled for <strong>4:45 PM CST</strong>. Add one hour to get <strong>5:45 PM EST</strong>. Keep the 45 minutes unchanged.</p><h3>Example 3: crossing midnight</h3><p>An event starts at <strong>11:30 PM CST on 22 September</strong>. Adding one hour gives <strong>12:30 AM EST on 23 September</strong>. The Eastern date is one day later. The calculator handles month-end and year-end changes too.</p></section>
<section><h2>Why is the difference one hour?</h2><p>UTC is a shared reference for comparing times. CST is UTC−06:00, and EST is UTC−05:00. At 6:00 PM UTC, these standard-time clocks show noon CST and 1:00 PM EST. Their offsets differ by one hour.</p><p>On this page, CST means <strong>North American Central Standard Time</strong>. It does not mean China Standard Time, which uses the same abbreviation but a different offset.</p></section>
<section><h2>CST vs CDT: check daylight saving time</h2><p>Standard time and daylight time have different names and UTC offsets:</p><div class="ce-tablewrap"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Time basis</th><th scope="col">Central</th><th scope="col">Eastern</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Standard</td><td>CST · UTC−06:00</td><td>EST · UTC−05:00</td></tr><tr><td>Daylight</td><td>CDT · UTC−05:00</td><td>EDT · UTC−04:00</td></tr></tbody></table></div><p style="margin-top:12px">The paired conversions CST → EST and CDT → EDT both add one hour. Mixing the labels gives different answers: CST → EDT adds two hours, while CDT → EST adds zero hours.</p><p>This calculator uses the pair you select. It does not look up city rules or change the mode based on the date. For a Chicago-to-New York meeting, confirm the local time-zone labels for the meeting date, especially around clock changes.</p></section>
<section><h2>Quick checks before sharing a time</h2><ul><li><strong>Check AM and PM:</strong> 12 AM is midnight; 12 PM is noon.</li><li><strong>Check the date:</strong> 11:00 PM–11:59:59 PM CST becomes the next day in EST.</li><li><strong>Keep minutes and seconds:</strong> 8:20:15 CST becomes 9:20:15 EST.</li><li><strong>Check the abbreviation:</strong> CST and CDT refer to different offsets.</li></ul></section>
